# Research

Canario uses **source Books plus transversal ledgers** for research that can affect
durable architecture.

A source Book studies one coherent external object — a standard, project, system,
institution, benchmark, paper lineage, or protocol family — using as many
primary/secondary URLs as that object actually requires. It is **not** one Book per
web page.

Cross-source conclusions are made only in a synthesis package after source Books
exist.

Central research navigation:

- `references.bib` — canonical machine-readable bibliography for papers/projects
  that materially influence architecture or benchmark design.
- `LITERATURE_MAP.md` — concise decision-oriented map of the live reading set.
- `PACKAGE_PROTOCOL.md` — evidence and synthesis rules.

The bibliography does not replace Source Books: a citation records identity; a Book
records what the source establishes, its limits, and what Canario may or may not
transfer.
