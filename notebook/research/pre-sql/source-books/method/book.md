# Research method and closure discipline

## Question

How do we know a research decision is deep enough to influence architecture?

## Deep-audit basis

Method evidence is local to prior Notebook/Plaza practice; no external product scar is required.

## Evidence horizon

- **AKS-S001 — Stereo Research Package Protocol:** Ledger-v1, bounded transfers, current-version rule
- **AKS-S002 — Plaza Principles:** Evidence before inference; provenance; identity; standards discipline
- **AKS-S003 — Plaza Correctness Audit Report:** Inventory != correctness; derived identity/segmentation can be wrong while raw evidence is sound
- **AKS-S053 — Stereo research transfer protocol:** Requires shared problem, mechanism, transfer and stop condition before cross-domain adoption
- **AKS-S054 — Stereo research ledger discipline:** CSV owns atoms/relationships while Markdown owns argument/synthesis; ledgerization is extract→verify→compact
- **AKS-S055 — Plaza quality and validation guidance:** Separates source audit, derived-record correctness and explicit validation checkpoints

## Claim ledger synopsis

- **AKS-C036:** A complete inventory of files does not prove correctness of derived structured records. **ActaKit:** ActaKit fixtures/tests must audit derived claims/identity/relations against artifacts, not only pipeline completion.
- **AKS-C037:** Identity errors propagate into downstream records and can make structurally valid data semantically wrong. **ActaKit:** Identity reconciliation requires explicit audit and provenance.
- **AKS-C039:** Research transfers need explicit stop conditions so external mechanisms do not become accidental architecture authority. **ActaKit:** Every adopted standard pattern in this Book has a non-transfer boundary.
- **AKS-C060:** A research horizon is complete when remaining gaps no longer threaten the pending decision, not when source count reaches a quota. **ActaKit:** Stop this Book at pre-SQL semantics; move unanswered implementation detail into fixtures/SQL study.
- **AKS-C072:** A cross-domain mechanism is transferable only when the shared problem, mechanism, bounded transfer and stop condition are explicit. **ActaKit:** Every standards-derived schema pressure must include a do-not-copy boundary and stop condition.
- **AKS-C073:** Research quality improves when atomic claims and relationships live in ledgers while Markdown is reserved for synthesis and rationale. **ActaKit:** Keep per-Book claim/source ledgers authoritative for research atoms; do not bury evidence in prose.

## Bounded transfer

Use Book-first, ledgers-first, bounded transfer, counterexamples and explicit stop conditions.

## Do not copy

Do not turn Notebook bookkeeping into product architecture or source-count theater.

## Schema pressure / expensive mistake avoided

Schema work is blocked until evidence claims, collisions and residual risks resolve the pending schema decisions.

## Residual risk

Method quality still depends on human judgment about whether a gap threatens the decision.

## Closure verdict

Deep-audited for the pre-SQL decision horizon. This Book contributes evidence and constraints; it does not independently authorize schema or implementation changes.
