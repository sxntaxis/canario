# ActaKit Notebook

The Notebook preserves research evidence, design exploration, and bounded transfers that inform ActaKit without becoming product authority by mere existence.

Current product/design authority remains under `docs/`. Research under `notebook/research/` may justify a later architectural change, but it does not silently change contracts or authorize implementation.


Post-SQL bounded implementation evidence that is not itself product authority may
live under `notebook/implementation/`. Current ingress proof:
`notebook/implementation/INGRESS_001.md`.
